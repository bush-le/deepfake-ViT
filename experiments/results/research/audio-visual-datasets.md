# Audio-Visual Deepfake Datasets Catalog & Analysis

---

## 1. Key Multi-Modal Deepfake Datasets
- **FakeAVCeleb**: 21.5K audio-visual deepfake clips combining video face swapping and audio voice cloning.
- **AV-Deepfake1M**: Large-scale benchmark covering multi-modal manipulation algorithms.
- **DFDC (Deepfake Detection Challenge)**: 128K video clips with diverse lighting, compression, and audio dubbing.

---

## 2. Role in Project
While the primary project focuses on visual image classification using Meta DINOv3 Vision Transformers, audio-visual datasets provide context for multi-modal extensions.
