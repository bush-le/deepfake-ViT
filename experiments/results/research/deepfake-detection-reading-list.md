# Deepfake Detection Core Reading List & Literature Review

---

## 1. Foundational Literature
- **DINOv3 (Meta AI, 2024)**: Vision Transformers with dense self-supervised pre-training, register tokens, and multi-scale attention.
- **Wang et al. (CNN-generated images are surprisingly easy to spot, CVPR 2020)**: Universal frequency artifacts in convolutional generative models.
- **Ojha et al. (Towards Universal Fake Image Detection, CVPR 2023)**: Pre-trained visual representations for cross-generator generalization.
- **Frank et al. (Leveraging Frequency Analysis for Deep Fake Image Recognition, ICML 2020)**: 2D FFT and DCT analysis of generative checkerboard artifacts.
