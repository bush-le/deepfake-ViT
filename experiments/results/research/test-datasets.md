# Evaluation Test Datasets Specification

---

## 1. Authoritative Coursework Test Benchmark Suites
1. **Test CourseWork Balanced (`test_coursework_44methods_balanced_zero_leakage.csv`)**:
   - **21,446 images** (10,723 Real vs. 10,723 Fake, exact 1:1 balance).
   - Uniform sampling of ~300 samples across all 44 methods.
2. **Test CourseWork Full Suite (`test_coursework_44methods_full_zero_leakage.csv`)**:
   - **50,084 images** (25,042 Real vs. 25,042 Fake, exact 1:1 balance).
   - Full-scale evaluation across all available held-out test frames.
